Things taken out of the prototype on purpose, kept here so they can be looked at again. Nothing here is decided. Taking something out is not a decision that it is wrong.

## P-001: "What to move" and "Save layout" chips under the room

- **What it was.** After the owner confirmed changes, two glass chips appeared above the prompt bar: "What to move" (opened the ordered move list) and "Save layout" (opened the save dialog).
- **Where it was.** The editor's bottom area, shown only after a confirm with nothing staged.
- **Why parked.** Removed from the editor on the owner's request, 2026-11-12, to keep the bottom area for the prompt bar and its cards.
- **Still there.** The save icon in the top-right bar still saves a layout. The move list (sheet `fn`) still opens when the AI links to it.

## P-002: "Save layout" and "What to move" chips in the Layouts sheet

- **What it was.** Two of the four chips under the layouts list (the others, Duplicate and New from the original room, stay).
- **Why parked.** Removed together with P-001 so the two entry points stay consistent.

## P-003: "Ask Kith" in the tap menu

- **What it was.** An AI icon with a check count in the object's tap menu, which opened the chat sheet on that object.
- **Why parked.** The prompt bar now takes the selection or tapped object as its context, so the button repeated it. The check marker now flies into the menu in its place.
